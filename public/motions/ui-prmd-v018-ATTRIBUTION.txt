Jun. https://www.idahofallshighered.org/vakanski/ui-prmd.html; https://zenodo.org/records/23104965. Data released under Open Data Commons Public Domain Dedication and License v1.0, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/pddl/1-0/. Changes: selected six complete right-arm Vicon episodes; five-frame marker smoothing; marker-based anatomical coordinate frame, segment directions and length-proxy normalization; 121-point time resampling and quantization. Mirroring the right arm for a game left arm is a derived adaptation, not a recorded bilateral or weighted exercise. No source finger motion or clinical joint centre, therapist approval, or Kinect forward kinematics is inferred.
